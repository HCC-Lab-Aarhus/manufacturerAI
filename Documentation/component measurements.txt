battery 2xAAA:

These are a bit special. The battery holder is just a printed plastic cavity, while the pins with springs are flat metal sheets we need to insert on either side. On one side, we have two disconnected pins (the negative and positive), while the other side has a flat plane, no pin connections, which connects the first battery to the second in series.

We have three metal plate components:

PIN SIDE (two separate small plates):
One plate with a raised dome contact (positive terminal of battery one), and one plate with a spring (negative terminal of battery two).
Dimensions for each small plate:
- Plate body: 1.46mm thick, 12.03mm wide, 12.1mm tall (rectangle).
- Center of plate has a raised dome contact, 7.5mm diameter.
- Bottom of plate has a rectangular tab extension: 7.5mm long, 0.3mm thick, 3mm wide. This tab passes through a slot in the holder floor to reach the ink traces below.

CONNECTOR SIDE (one wide plate):
One wide plate with both a spring and a dome contact, bridging the two cells in series.
Dimensions:
- Plate body: 1.46mm thick, 27.2mm wide, 12mm tall (rectangle).
- Two dome contacts, each 7.5mm diameter, spaced 8.7mm apart (center-to-center).
- Each dome sits 2mm from the nearest side wall.
- Between the two domes there is a ~1.2mm gap where the cell divider wall sits.
- Bottom of plate has a rectangular tab extension: 7.5mm tall, 0.3mm thick, 3mm wide. Same purpose as above.

WALL LAYOUT (connector side, looking from outside):
  [ side wall (2mm) | dome 1 (7.5mm) | cell divider (~1.2mm) | dome 2 (7.5mm) | side wall (2mm) ]
  Total internal width: ~20.2mm
  Dome 1 center: 5.75mm from left wall
  Dome 2 center: 14.45mm from left wall (= 5.75 + 8.7)

CUTOUTS NEEDED:
- Plate channels on each side of the holder wall, sized to fit the plates snugly.
- Dome contact windows through the side walls and cell divider so the 7.5mm domes can poke through and touch the batteries.
- Pin slot through the floor for each tab extension, connecting to the ink traces.
